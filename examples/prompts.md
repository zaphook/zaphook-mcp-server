# Example prompts

Ask in any language. The assistant picks the tools.

## Customer service

- "Which conversations are unread right now? Summarize what each customer wants."
- "Read my conversation with 5511999998888 and draft a reply. Don't send it yet."
- "Is the 24-hour window still open with this contact?"
- "Send *Your order has shipped and arrives tomorrow* to Maria." *(the assistant shows the recipient and text and waits for your confirmation)*
- "The window is closed. Which approved template fits a payment reminder? Send it with her name."

## Templates

- "List my approved templates and their variables."
- "Why was the `promo_black_friday` template rejected?"

## Rich messages

- "Send a message with three buttons to 5511999998888: *Track order*, *Talk to an agent*, *Cancel*."
- "Send our store location to this customer: latitude -23.5614, longitude -46.6559, name *Loja Paulista*."
- "Send this catalog: https://example.com/catalog.pdf, file name *catalog.pdf*."

## Operations and monitoring

- "Check the health of all my numbers. Is any quality rating not green?"
- "Show the events that failed in the last 24 hours and explain why."
- "Did the message I sent to this customer get delivered?"

## Setup

- "List my channels and their destinations."
- "Pause the destination *CRM* on my Support channel."
- "Create a destination on my Support channel that posts to https://my-app.example.com/webhook."
